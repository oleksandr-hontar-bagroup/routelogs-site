The problem

Coordinating sample collection by hand is fragile, slow, and invisible.

Spreadsheets, phone calls, and paper manifests can't keep up with dozens of clinics, multiple couriers, and time-sensitive samples. When something slips, nobody finds out until it's too late.

Routes planned by guessworkCouriers double back, miss pickups, and burn hours that the schedule can't absorb.

No visibility for the clientLabs and clinics can't see what was collected, what wasn't, or where a sample is right now.

Sample integrity left to chanceCooling and temperature go unmonitored, putting specimen quality — and compliance — at risk.

Hours → minutes

Route planning that used to take a dispatcher all morning is generated automatically.

100% visible

Every checkpoint, pickup, and exception — tracked and shared in real time across the chain.

FAQ

Questions, answered

How long does it take to get up and running?

It depends on your size and volume. A single territory with one or two routes can be live in a matter of days. Larger operations — multiple hubs, dispatchers, and many clients — are introduced in phases over a few weeks. We map setup to your volume and migrate your existing schedule together with you.

Do we have to replace our couriers or vehicles?

No. RouteLogs runs your existing couriers and routes — it plans and tracks the work, it doesn't replace your fleet. Your drivers simply pick up the native courier app and follow the optimized route.

Is there a mobile app for couriers?

Yes — a native app built specifically for RouteLogs. It guides each pickup in order, captures chain-of-custody at every stop, and syncs status back to dispatchers and the client portal in real time.

How is our data protected?

RouteLogs is GDPR-aligned, hosted on AWS, and encrypted in transit and at rest, with role-based access and full audit logging. See the security section for the full picture.

What about cold-chain and temperature-sensitive samples?

RouteLogs is cold-chain aware today: it tracks cooling-container availability and records temperature conditions along the route. Direct temperature-sensor integration for continuous, automatic monitoring is on the roadmap.

Can our clients see their own samples?

Yes. Every customer gets a secure portal showing real-time checkpoint status — what was picked up, what wasn't, and where any problem occurred — plus instant exception alerts.

How much does it cost?

We're finalizing public pricing. While we're pre-launch, founding partners get a locked-in founding rate, and plans scale with your routes, couriers, and clients. Request a demo and we'll put a number to your operation.

We run on spreadsheets and phone calls today — is switching hard?

We onboard with you. We import your clinics and current schedule, run alongside your existing process, and switch over only once your team is confident. No big-bang cutover.
