Privacy Policy

Last updated 4 June 2026

Pre-launch template. This policy is written to be GDPR-aligned, but it must be reviewed and tailored by qualified counsel before you go live.

RouteLogs ("RouteLogs", "we", "us") builds software that automates medical specimen logistics — from the clinic where samples are collected to the lab where they are processed. This policy explains what personal data we collect through this website, why we collect it, and the rights you have over it. It covers visitors to this site and people who request a demo; it does not cover the specimen data our customers process inside the platform, which is governed by a separate data-processing agreement.

1. Who is responsible for your data

RouteLogs is the data controller for personal data collected through this website. For any privacy question, or to exercise your rights, contact us at hello@routelogs.app.

2. What we collect

  • Information you give us. When you request a demo we collect your name, company, and work email. If you book a time, our scheduling provider also processes the details you enter into the booking form.
  • Communications. If you email us or otherwise get in touch, we keep the content of that correspondence.
  • Technical data. Like most websites, our hosting infrastructure records standard server logs (such as IP address, browser type, and the pages requested) to keep the service secure and reliable.

3. Why we use it, and our lawful basis

  • To respond to demo requests and talk to you about RouteLogs — on the basis of taking steps at your request prior to entering into a contract, and our legitimate interest in growing the business.
  • To operate and secure the website — on the basis of our legitimate interest in keeping the service available and protected against abuse.
  • To comply with legal obligations where applicable.

We do not use the site to build advertising profiles, and we do not sell personal data.

4. Service providers we share with

We share personal data only with the processors that help us run the site and respond to you. Each is bound by appropriate contractual safeguards.

ProviderPurpose
Amazon Web Services (AWS)Cloud hosting and infrastructure
CalendlyScheduling the demo call (loaded only when you choose to book)
Email providerReceiving and replying to your enquiry

5. International transfers

Some providers may process data outside your country or the EEA. Where that happens, we rely on appropriate safeguards — such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses — to protect your data.

6. How long we keep it

We keep demo-request and contact details for as long as needed to follow up and, if you become a customer, for the life of that relationship plus any period required by law. Server logs are kept for a short, rolling period. When data is no longer needed, we delete or anonymise it.

7. Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to our processing of your personal data, and the right to data portability. You can also lodge a complaint with your local data-protection authority. To exercise any of these rights, email hello@routelogs.app.

8. Cookies

This site does not set advertising or tracking cookies. Strictly necessary cookies may be used to keep the site working. If you choose to open the scheduler, Calendly may set its own cookies — see Calendly's privacy notice for details.

9. Security

We protect personal data with encryption in transit and at rest, role-based access controls, and audit logging, hosted on hardened AWS infrastructure. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your data and to notify you and the relevant authorities of any breach as required by law.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the product and our practices evolve. We will revise the "last updated" date above and, for material changes, take additional steps where required.

11. Contact

Questions about privacy? Email hello@routelogs.app.
